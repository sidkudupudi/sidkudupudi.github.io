About

Sidharth Kudupudi

Engineer · Minneapolis, MN

I'm an engineer with an M.S. in Robotics from the University of Minnesota, originally trained in mechatronics. I like the whole arc of a problem: making sense of messy data, building something that works, then making it fast and dependable enough that someone else can rely on it.

I care about evidence more than demos. I test on data a system has never seen, put the unflattering number next to the flattering one, and write down what didn't work.

How I work

  1. 01

    Measure on data the model never saw

    Held-out sequences, cross-dataset tests and variant-held-out folds, reported next to the flattering number.

  2. 02

    Report the trivial baseline

    Accuracy means little without the majority-class score beside it; false alarms belong next to recall.

  3. 03

    A port must match its prototype

    Stage-by-stage parity tests before quoting a speed-up.

  4. 04

    Say where it fails

    Every case study has a failures section with real examples, not just limitations in the abstract.
