Multi-camera BEV perception: Lift-Splat with DINOv2

I built a Lift-Splat bird's-eye-view network with a DINOv2 foundation-model backbone, module by module, and verified its six-camera geometry end to end on real nuScenes calibration.

01

Problem & constraints

Camera-only BEV perception is attractive because cameras are cheap, but lifting 2D features into 3D requires per-pixel depth reasoning and an exact camera rig model.

02

My contribution

Architecture and geometry implementation

  • Implemented the Lift, Geometry and Splat modules with shape tests.
  • Replaced the image encoder with DINOv2 ViT-S/14 patch tokens.
  • Verified the geometry with the real nuScenes calibration of all six cameras.

Failures, limits & next steps

Next steps · what would prove it

  • Extension: rasterise drivable area, lanes and 3D box centres into BEV targets.
  • Extension: train with DINOv2 frozen, then unfreeze the last blocks.
  • What would prove it: BEV IoU and nuScenes mAP / NDS on v1.0-trainval.
