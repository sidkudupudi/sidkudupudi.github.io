01

Problem & constraints

Early smoke detection matters most when plumes are small and faint, and labelled data for every camera and season does not exist. Foundation models promise text-prompted segmentation with no training; their claims need checking against ground truth.

02

My contribution

Pipeline, ground-truth scoring, prompt experiments and TensorRT export

  • Built the text → boxes → masks pipeline (Grounding DINO Swin-T → SAM ViT-H).
  • Scored detections against the 744 Pascal-VOC smoke boxes: recall, hit@IoU, precision, AP50.
  • Ran the prompt experiments and the cross-phrase NMS fix.
  • Exported the MobileSAM encoder to ONNX/TensorRT and timed it.
  • Removed an unsupported claim from the original demo video (see failures).

Failures, limits & next steps

✕Faint plumes

A faint plume is missed and the sky is boxed as 'haze' instead.

✕Tiny, distant plumes

Small plumes far from the camera are missed.

✕Whole-sky boxes

A box covering the entire sky swallows the plume.

✕A demo claim that did not hold

The original video drew a smoke 'trajectory' and overlaid '232 FPS'. The 744 images come from many cameras and days, so consecutive frames are unrelated, and 232 FPS was the MobileSAM encoder alone. The video is no longer presented as a result.

Next steps · what would prove it

  • A real HPWREN time-lapse sequence for temporal tracking.
  • End-to-end TensorRT pipeline including the grounding model.
  • Per-distance recall to quantify small-plume performance.
